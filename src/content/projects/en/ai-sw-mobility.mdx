## AEB pipeline

The ROS 2 AEB work included generating a center path from cone boundaries, latching a stop at the red entry, and maintaining stateful fail-safe behavior. These functions connect the perceived cone corridor to tracking and braking commands.

## Stanley tracking

I implemented Stanley-based path tracking for cone-corridor driving. The work included steering constraints, steering-rate limits, and motion-preview compensation.

The next integrated trial will examine tracking behavior with the control team's steering-delay correction in place.

## Simulation & debugging

Gazebo provided the closed-loop environment used to validate the AEB pipeline. Foxglove was used to visualize and debug the generated path, steering behavior, and stop state.

The simulation work and vehicle tests are reported separately because they exercise different conditions. No simulation speed or tracking-error metric is presented as a real-vehicle result.
