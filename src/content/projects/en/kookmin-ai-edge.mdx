## Learning pipeline and evaluation

I built the workflow from driving-data collection to model training, TorchScript export, and ROS 2 inference. Development included switching between learning-based driving and deterministic traffic-light or mission handling. **The final competition strategy used rule-based driving after steering oscillation remained in the learning-based approach.**

## Engineering lessons and next measurements

The team used rule-based driving in the event because learned steering continued to oscillate. Perception and hardware constraints also informed the shift from using both white and yellow lane markings to focusing on yellow markings.

The next useful analysis is a time-aligned record of sensor input, predicted steering, issued command, and vehicle response. That would help distinguish model behavior from command filtering and actuator delay. Repeated runs on the same route would then connect offline model errors with actual trajectory stability.
