## Learning pipeline and evaluation

I built the workflow from driving-data collection to model training, TorchScript export, and ROS 2 inference. Development included switching between learning-based driving and deterministic traffic-light or mission handling. **The final competition strategy used the integrated rule-based driving controller.**

The steering policy combines a **ResNet18 image encoder** with a **LiDAR 1D CNN**:

I compared steering predictions with recorded commands offline and examined steering response during vehicle trials. This connected model evaluation with the command and controller behavior observed on the vehicle.

## Perception and dataset workflow

I prepared and reviewed datasets in Roboflow, including annotation, class organization, data splits, augmentation, and export. I trained **YOLOv8n and YOLO11n** object detectors and selected a model based on validation **mAP50 and mAP50–95**. Detection work covered traffic lights, vehicles, obstacles, and cones.

| Component | Model or method | Evaluation focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learned steering | ResNet18 + LiDAR 1D CNN | Steering prediction against recorded commands |
| Object detection | YOLOv8n and YOLO11n | Validation mAP50 and mAP50–95 |
| Deployment | TorchScript inference in ROS 2 | Connection between policy output and vehicle commands |

## Engineering integration

The team used rule-based driving in the competition. Vehicle integration brought together mission priorities, drive gating, perception, and steering commands. Perception and hardware considerations also informed the focus on yellow lane markings.

My work spanned the development cycle: collecting and annotating data, training and evaluating models, connecting ROS 2 mission modules, and tuning the vehicle for the driving and parking events.

## What I learned

The learned policy drove smoothly in the simulator. Moving from simulation toward vehicle operation highlighted the importance of **training-data coverage and quality** and of examining how perception, decision-making, and control interact.

Working across model training and ROS 2 integration gave me a practical view of the full driving pipeline: how data becomes a prediction, how a mission selects an action, and how that action reaches the vehicle.
