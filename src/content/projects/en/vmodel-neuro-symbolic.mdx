## What I tried

The project combined ROS 2 decision-making and waypoint-following components with CARLA. My implementation covered Pure Pursuit for path tracking and longitudinal speed control, alongside the integration work required to connect these components to the simulation.

This was an early learning project. The goal was to establish a working driving loop, but the completed project did not achieve stable autonomous driving.

## What failed

**Stable autonomous driving was not achieved.** Closed-loop experiments exposed unstable path tracking that persisted through the project's adjustments.

The project's completed status refers to the end of the Dream Semester project. It does not mean the driving objective was successfully completed.

## Integration work

ROS 2 decision and control components were connected with CARLA for closed-loop experiments. The contribution record also includes later MCU bridge adaptation, without claiming that it resolved the original tracking problem or established successful hardware driving.

## What I learned

The project established foundational experience in ROS 2, CARLA, path tracking, and control integration. Its value lies in the implementation and experimental work, alongside an explicit record of the unsuccessful outcome.

The later projects continued this trajectory through Gazebo testing, vehicle integration, and more structured mission logic. The portfolio records those later results on their own pages rather than retroactively treating them as success in this project.
